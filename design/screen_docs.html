ROSTERING & SHIFTS • Section 4.2

Shifts, Schedules & Grace Period Resolution

schedule Updated October 2026 • 4 min read • Target: Enterprise v2026.2+

AuraPass sovereign on-prem edge inferencing reconciles sub-300ms facial recognition timestamps against active shift boundaries locally on node clusters. Rather than relying on cloud round-trips to evaluate attendance status, edge workers evaluate punch validity against deterministic roster schedules in microsecond intervals.

When a subject presents at an access node, their 512-dimensional zero-knowledge vector is reconciled against the enrolled partition. Concurrently, the exact ingest time instant is tested against shift definitions, applying grace window arithmetic prior to triggering dry contacts and dispatching authenticated state payloads.

Mathematical Model tag

Grace buffers guarantee operational resilience across heavy worker shift changeovers. An arrival punch $T_{\text{arrival}}$ is classified as timely using strict sub-second comparator boundaries:

T_effective = T_arrival <= (T_shift_start + T_grace) Deterministic
info
Note: Grace buffers are evaluated at the local edge node before any webhook or payroll dispatch is emitted, preventing transient network lag from penalizing employees.

Worked Grace Period Matrix tag

The state machine reconciles punch events into immutable attendance codes. Below is an evaluation breakdown for a standard 09:00:00 morning shift across varied arrival stamps:

Shift Start Grace Buffer Actual Ingest Punch Status Recorded Payroll Dispatch Tag
09:00:00 15 min 08:54:12 ON TIME (EARLY) ON_TIME_PUNCH
09:00:00 15 min 09:12:44 ON TIME (GRACE) ON_TIME_WITHIN_GRACE
09:00:00 15 min 09:18:03 LATE (+3m) TARDY_EXCEEDED_GRACE
09:00:00 0 min (Strict) 09:01:10 LATE (+1m) TARDY_ZERO_TOLERANCE
verified_user
Important: Turnstile relay dry-contacts fire immediately upon valid biometric match (sub-300ms) regardless of grace status; late arrivals are logged to timesheet databases but do not block entry unless physical lockout rules are enabled.

Turnstile & Relay Behavior tag

Physical turnstile solenoids operate on independent micro-controller hardware watchdog loops. This guarantees zero perceptible pedestrian latency at high throughput perimeter checkpoints. Biometric authorization and attendance classification remain decoupled at the physical layer.

warning
Warning: Shifts crossing midnight (24:00) require ISO 8601 full UTC epoch timestamps in your roster sync payload to prevent roll-over index collisions.

Configuration Reference tag

Declare shift policies within your node's declarative configuration manifest or stream updates via the gRPC Rostering Bridge:

  "shift_id": "shift_morn_tier1",
